About the data

Reading the cells

Plain values come from the sources listed in each site profile (select a site name). Values with a half-filled dot are estimates, unit conversions (acres, square feet) or general knowledge; the TRL range, hardware share and city population are editorial assessments throughout. n/p means the figure was not found in public sources, n/a that it does not apply to the site type.

Normalised metrics

Floor-area density (m² per ha), people density (current and target, per ha), people per company and floor area per person are computed from the numeric fields. Hover a value to see the inputs; a ratio inherits the half-filled dot if one of its inputs is an estimate.

Orchestration depth (A12)

An editorial scale for how far a site goes beyond providing space. Level 1: space and encounter (shared spaces, events, community management). Level 2: plus curated programmes or membership. Level 3: plus active brokering, where staff or formats match specific partners to specific problems. Level 4: plus a standing framework for joint projects, such as enrolment or consortium contracts, an association acting as project sponsor, or an operator acting as lead applicant.

Caveats

Boundaries differ: some rows describe one building (Station F, ARENA2036), others a whole district (Adlershof, 22@). Reference years range from 2015 to 2026. Hard-infrastructure data such as grid capacity in MVA, floor loads in kN/m² or crane capacity is rarely published; those n/p cells are the questions to put to operators directly.
